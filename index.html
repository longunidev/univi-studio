UniVi Studio

UniVi Studio is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. It helps you turn an idea into a short animated video (hand-drawn cartoon, whiteboard, stick figure or infographic style), then share the finished video to your own social media accounts.

How sharing works

  • You connect your own YouTube, Facebook Page, Instagram and TikTok accounts through each platform's official sign-in page (OAuth). The app never sees your password.
  • You choose a finished video, write the caption for each account and approve the post. Nothing is published without your approval.
  • The app uses only the official APIs of each platform, limits how many posts each account makes per day, and spaces posts out.
  • For TikTok, the video is sent to your TikTok inbox as a draft. You open the TikTok app, review it and publish it yourself.
  • Access tokens are stored only on your computer. You can disconnect an account at any time, which revokes the app's access.
