Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: November 16, 2026

Summary

UniVi Studio has no server of its own. Everything it receives from Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok (account IDs, names, pictures and access tokens) is stored only on your own computer. The developer never receives or keeps a copy, so there is nothing to delete on the developer's side. You can delete all of it yourself with the steps below.

1. Disconnect the account in the app

  1. Open UniVi Studio and go to Đăng mạng xã hội (Social posting) › Tài khoản (Accounts).
  2. Click Ngắt kết nối (Disconnect) next to the Facebook Page, Instagram account, YouTube channel or TikTok account.

The app revokes the access token where the platform supports it and deletes the token and account details from your computer.

2. Remove the app from Facebook and Instagram

  1. On Facebook, open Settings & privacy › Settings › Business integrations (or Apps and websites).
  2. Find UniVi Studio and click Remove.
  3. Tick the option to delete posts, videos and events the app published if you also want those removed, then confirm.

This stops the app from accessing your Facebook Pages and connected Instagram accounts. To remove access for other platforms: TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services) and Google Account › Third-party connections.

3. Delete the data stored on your computer

Quit the app, then delete its data folder:

  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/UniVi

This removes all projects, rendered videos, post history and any remaining tokens. If you turned on Google Drive sync, also delete the UniVi Studio folder in your Google Drive.

4. Published posts

Videos and posts already published stay on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok until you delete them there (or choose to delete them in step 2).

5. Need help?

Email longuni.ent@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion. We reply within 30 days and confirm once nothing related to your account remains.
